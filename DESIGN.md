# VYBORA — Design system

- Audience: guest viewers, creators and communities.
- Primary job: let people discover and watch vertical video immediately, then join when they want to interact.
- Palette: Midnight #05050C, Deep Panel #10101C, Violet #9B55FF, Aqua #50F0D0, Magenta #FF4FC8, White #FBFAFF.
- Typography: system/Inter-style sans for fast, compact mobile reading; heavy display wordmark.
- Layout: full-height snap video feed, slim branded header, contextual desktop side panels, persistent mobile navigation.
- Signature: the V mark uses an aqua-to-violet-to-magenta energy gradient; feed controls float over the video with translucent rings.
- Behavior contract: CSS-only visual layer. Existing guest browsing, Supabase auth, posts, reactions, bookmarks, follows, comments, search, live, messaging and data-saver behavior remain unchanged.
- Accessibility: keyboard focus rings, semantic existing controls, touch-sized actions, reduced-motion support and mobile safe-area padding.
